# Photography Shot List and Image Specs

Photography is the most important input to the website. Consistency across products matters more than any single beautiful photo.

## Overall look

- Natural, directional daylight (window light from one side). Soft, real shadows.
- Backgrounds: warm neutral — limestone, linen, plaster, light oak. Same background family for all hero shots.
- Warm, slightly muted colour grade. No heavy filters, no pure-white "Amazon" backgrounds.
- Leave space around the object — don't crop tight.

## Per product (minimum)

| #   | Shot              | Ratio        | Notes                                                                         |
| --- | ----------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Hero**          | 4:5 portrait | Front/three-quarter view, centred, neutral background. Used on product cards. |
| 2   | **Angle 2**       | 4:5          | Side or top-down. Used as the hover image on cards.                           |
| 3   | **Angle 3**       | 4:5          | Back or underside (shows finish, felt pads, etc.).                            |
| 4   | **Texture macro** | 4:5 or 1:1   | Close-up of veining / glaze / surface. Sharp focus.                           |
| 5   | **Scale**         | 4:5          | In a hand, or beside a known object (book, cup) so the size is obvious.       |
| 6   | **Lifestyle**     | 4:5 and 16:9 | Styled in a real interior — shelf, console, dining table.                     |

**One-of-a-kind pieces:** every photo must be of _that exact piece_, because the customer receives it.
**Variants (size/finish):** at least a hero shot for each variant.

## Brand / editorial shots (for the homepage and About page)

- 2–3 wide interior scenes featuring several pieces (16:9, landscape) — homepage hero candidates
- 3–5 material close-ups (raw stone, clay, tools, hands at work — only if real)
- Studio or founder portraits (optional)
- Optional: a 6–10 second silent loop video (slow light movement over a piece) for the hero

## Technical specs

- Minimum **3000 px** on the long edge; JPEG quality 90+ or TIFF
- sRGB colour profile
- Consistent white balance across a session (use a grey card)
- File names: `<product-slug>-01-hero.jpg`, `<product-slug>-02-angle.jpg`, `…-04-texture.jpg`, `…-05-scale.jpg`, `…-06-lifestyle.jpg`
- Deliver one folder per product; folder name = product slug (same as the `slug` column in the product template)
